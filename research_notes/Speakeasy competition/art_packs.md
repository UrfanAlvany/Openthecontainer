## TL;DR: what to buy

**One art style: Synty POLYGON.** It is the stylised low-poly look used by many commercial sims. It is consistent across 170+ packs, and every POLYGON character uses the same Humanoid (Mecanim) skeleton, so Mixamo and Synty animations work on all of them. No Synty pack is set in the 1920s. But four packs that are on sale right now cover a speakeasy, café, cellar and brick street well, and their "town man / sheriff / bartender / cop" characters pass as 1920s men.

Gaps to fill, cheapest first:
1. **Women's 1920s outfits (biggest gap).**
   - Short term: recolour the Nightclubs and Casino dresses through Synty's atlas textures and add hats.
   - Proper fix: commission 4–6 custom outfits on the POLYGON skeleton from a "Synty-style" freelancer. Price unknown **[unverified]**.
   - Synty's modular Sidekick packs are modern clothing only and $199.99 each, so they don't solve this.
2. **Art deco trim kit.** Sunburst panels, chevron mouldings, a mirrored back-bar, deco sconces and gold edging. These are simple meshes and decals; a day of Blender work. (Or the Casino pack later.)
3. **Moonshine still, double bass, candlestick phone, cathedral radio, gas lamp post.** Five simple props. Model them in the POLYGON style (flat colour from a palette texture).
4. **First-person arms.** We are first-person and none of the packs include FP arms. Use a POLYGON character's arm mesh with a period sleeve on a separate camera layer.
5. **Hands.** POLYGON characters have simplified, mitten-like hands. Glasses and bottles must be parented to the hand bone, and drinking or pouring animations may need small offsets **[unverified per pack]**. One Synty Idles review complains the crash-test dummy has only three fingers.
6. **Anachronisms to strip.** In City: modern cars, traffic lights, billboards, AC units. In Nightclubs: neon, lasers, DJ gear, TVs. In Shops: arcade, gym and electronics.

## Unity 6.3 / URP notes (read before importing)

- Every Synty pack page says: "Compatible with Unity 2022.3+, Supports URP and Built-in, Characters set up with Mecanim (no animations included)". The packages were built with 2022.3.56f1. Unity 6.3 is not named, but the 2026 updates suggest they work in newer versions. For example, City v1.12.5 "Fixed Serialized file format issue in newer Unity versions" (Sep 2026). **[verified on store; actual 6.3 import not tested]**
- **URP is a separate import step.** Reviews of Nightclubs (Nov and Dec 2025) say you must import the URP `.unitypackage` that ships inside the pack. One reviewer who tried converting by hand "failed at the custom shaders". Do not run Unity's Render Pipeline Converter on Synty materials. Use Synty's URP package (guide linked from each store page: bit.ly/synty-urp). **[verified: reviews]**
- Since the v1.9 updates (2025–2026), the Synty packs use Shader Graph shaders, share a "Generics" folder, and include a lighting and post-processing setup.
- **Flovi car packs are Built-in only.** They were made in Unity 2018.4 with the Standard shader and were last updated in Dec 2020. Converting them is a simple Render Pipeline Converter material upgrade **[unverified: no reviews exist]**.
- **The art alone won't fix the "cheap/toy" look.** Synty's store screenshots get their look from lighting: warm tungsten point lights, baked GI or light probes, fog, bloom, ACES tonemapping and colour grading. Budget a lighting pass. **[judgement]**

## Licences

- **Unity Asset Store:** Standard Unity EULA. Art assets are licensed to the buyer; only "Tools" are per-seat **[from memory, not re-read today]**.
- **Synty Store one-time purchase:** 5 seats.
- **Humble Synty bundle:** same terms as a Synty one-time purchase, but **1 seat**. Redeem the code on the Synty Store before it expires, then download the Unity packages from "My Downloads". **[verified: search snippet of Synty's Humble FAQ]** One seat is fine while Urfan is the only person running Unity.
- **SyntyPass ($40/month, 3-month minimum, whole library): not recommended.** After you cancel, you "must cease using our Assets in any new Intellectual Property", and a released game may only get "minor bug fixes" if "the Product is not substantially developed". Post-launch updates would therefore require staying subscribed for the life of the game. **[verified: Synty Standard Subscription Licence]**
