# Co-op multiplayer in job / business simulators and small-team hits, 2023–2026

Researched 3 Oct 2026. Sources: Steam store pages, SteamCharts / SteamDB monthly data, GameDiscoverCo, Photon / Steamworks / Unity docs, Steam community threads, press. Player counts are Steam concurrent users (CCU), not sales. Anything marked **[estimate]**, **[unverified]** or **[snippet only]** should not be quoted as fact. "Snippet only" means it came from a search-result summary whose page I could not open.

---

## Q1. Which job/business sims have co-op, and how did co-op affect sales, CCU, reviews and streamer coverage?

### Takeaway
Co-op is not required for a sim to be a hit. TCG Card Shop Simulator went to 1.0 single-player-only and is Overwhelmingly Positive with about 3.5M players. Gas Station Simulator never added co-op. But sims built for co-op from day one have the biggest viral ceilings and the longest tails. Supermarket Together, a free 16-player supermarket game, now holds about 2× the CCU of the paid Supermarket Simulator it resembles. Co-op added after launch gave a one-month bump (Supermarket Simulator) or a modest lasting lift when paired with a free weekend (House Flipper 2).

### Cited Findings

### Inferences
- **Two models of sim success.**
  - **Watch-me-collect solo sims** (TCG Card Shop, Supermarket Simulator, Gas Station Sim) sell on the loop itself and on streamer "pack-opening/shop" content.
  - **Chaos-together sims** (Supermarket Together, Schedule I) sell through friend-group word of mouth.
  - The second model has a higher ceiling and a longer tail. Supermarket Together's 2026 monthly peaks (~10–12k) are about 2× Supermarket Simulator's (~5k), even though Supermarket Simulator had the bigger launch. Caveat: Supermarket Together is free, and CCU is not revenue.
- **Retrofitted co-op is a re-engagement event, not a relaunch.** Supermarket Simulator's co-op month nearly doubled its peak, then decayed to the pre-update level within about 2 months. House Flipper 2 got a bigger, longer lift, but the free weekend is a confound.
- Co-op adds to a working loop; it does not rescue a weak one. TCG Card Shop Simulator proves a single-player sim with a strong reveal and collect loop can be a top-tier hit, which is directly relevant to Dockside's pack-opening-style reveal.
- When a big solo sim lacks co-op, the demand gets filled by co-op-first clones (Supermarket Together). Bad clones fail (TCG Multiplayer clones). Unmet co-op demand on a hit is a competitive opening for others.

### Gaps
- No public revenue or sales deltas tied to any co-op update; only CCU proxies. I found no developer statement quantifying co-op's sales effect for PowerWash, House Flipper 2 or Supermarket Simulator.
- PowerWash 1 sales split by co-op vs solo is unknown, and the "17M players" figure is unconfirmed.
- No internet-cafe sim with notable co-op turned up. I found no data on Internet Cafe Simulator 1/2 co-op.
- Streamer-hours comparison (co-op vs solo sims): no reliable public dataset found.
- AppMagic article bodies are JS-rendered and could not be read, so their numbers come from search snippets only.

## Q2. What did the friendslop hits teach about why playing a job with friends is fun, and which ingredients fit a business sim?

### Takeaway
The friendslop hits are almost all jobs: scrap-collection quotas (Lethal Company, R.E.P.O.), filming content for views (Content Warning), climbing (PEAK) and dealing (Schedule I). The fun is not the job. It is the shared, physical, failure-prone execution of the job, heard through proximity voice and clipped for streams. Ingredients that transfer well to a business sim:
- role splitting
- difficulty that scales with player count
- physical object handling where things can go wrong
- a shared quota or stake
- a watchable "reveal" moment

Proximity voice only transfers if the game is a 3D walk-around space.

### Cited Findings

**Definition and traits**
- Wikipedia's list of friendslop traits:
  - low entry difficulty
  - proximity chat with lip-sync
  - low-fidelity 3D and physics interactions
  - priced under $20
  - team goals
  - often extraction loops (Lethal Company, R.E.P.O.)
  - "the game would be unenjoyable when played alone"
- Wikipedia also records the pushback on the term: House House dislikes the "carelessness" connotation of "slop", while Aggro Crab embraced it. — [Wikipedia: Friendslop](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Friendslop)
- The format is "low friction, quick buy-in, and zero pressure to stay for months", built around "moments worth sharing… scenes that look just as good in a TikTok clip or a stream highlight." **[snippet only]** — [AppMagic](https://appmagic.rocks/blog/friendslop-steam-games-2025)

**Lethal Company** (Zeekerss, solo dev)
- About 10M+ copies and $100M+ at $10 **[estimate, Jan 2024]**.
- CCU grew from ~611 at launch to ~43,268 by week 3 through word of mouth.
- Zeekerss calls it "a game about laughing at death": a system "that generates stories", where fright is followed by laughter, with "F— around and find out" play that suits streamers.
- Proximity voice, with walkie-talkies to extend range. — [Push To Talk](https://www.pushtotalk.gg/p/how-lethal-company-sold-10-million-copies)
- Voice is proximity-limited by default, with room reverb. The best moments come from information asymmetry, e.g. the person on the ship's radar warning a crewmate who cannot see the monster. **[snippet only]** — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Lethal_Company)

**R.E.P.O.** (semiwork)
- Built on Photon PUN plus Photon Voice. The devs said the voice plugin "is also very important to create fun moments."
- About 8.7M copies sold in March 2025 alone (Alinea Analytics, cited by Photon).
- The physics sync was the hardest part. — [Photon blog, 15 Apr 2025](https://blog.photonengine.com/r-e-p-o-multiplayer-success-powered-by-photon/)
- A dev quote that the concept shifted toward co-op because "Co-op cleaning in real life, it's not that fun" and nothing in the loop "kept players together" is **[snippet only; the PC Gamer article would not load]**. — [PC Gamer](https://www.pcgamer.com/games/horror/lets-just-fail-quickly-this-time-semiwork-took-a-big-risk-on-repo-after-its-first-game-took-6-years-to-make-and-didnt-sell-very-well/)

**PEAK** (Aggro Crab, 3 devs + Landfall, 4 devs)
- Sold 4.5M in under a month at $5–$8. Launched with only 29,384 wishlists, with its Steam page live about 4 days before launch.
- Median playtime 3h53m. Built in a one-month jam in Seoul plus remote work.
- One central mechanic, the damageable stamina bar, which "naturally led to basically every other feature".
- Proximity voice drives helping hands, revives and "silly roleplay". It is "a genuine joy to watch other people play": emotes, voice chaos, and a ghost form for dead players. — [GameDiscoverCo, 8 Jul 2025](https://newsletter.gamediscover.co/p/how-peak-sold-45m-copies-in-less)

**Content Warning** (Landfall)
- Free for its first 24h on 1 Apr 2024. Peaked at 204,439 CCU with 6.2–6.6M claims, then sold 1M copies at $7.99.
- The job: film scary things and upload the footage to go viral. — [Game Developer](https://www.gamedeveloper.com/business/content-warning-opens-to-near-205k-players-nets-6-2m-owners-at-launch); [Neowin](https://www.neowin.net/news/content-warning-still-sold-1-million-copies-on-steam-after-it-gave-away-66-million-for-free/)

**Business-sim examples of the same ingredients**
- Supermarket Together's design centres on scaling demand with player count and splitting roles (7 staff roles), which forces teams to divide the work. — [Steam](https://store.steampowered.com/app/2709570/Supermarket_Together/); [PCGamesN](https://www.pcgamesn.com/supermarket-together/steam)
- Job Simulator: Human Relations pitches co-op office work as "physics comedy… work together — or don't". — [Steam](https://store.steampowered.com/app/4965500/Job_Simulator_Human_Relations/)
- FuturLab framed PowerWash co-op as "a relaxing non-competitive environment". This is the cozy, parallel-play alternative to chaos co-op. — [GamesRadar](https://www.gamesradar.com/powerwash-simulator-co-op/)

### Inferences
How each ingredient fits a business sim, and Dockside in particular:

| Ingredient | Fit for a business sim | Dockside angle |
|---|---|---|
| Role splitting | Strong | e.g. one player bids at auction, one scrapes tiles, one appraises/sells. Mirrors Supermarket Together's roles. |
| Difficulty scales with players | Strong | More crew means more or larger containers per shift, so co-op never trivialises the economy. |
| Shared quota / stake | Strong | Lethal Company's quota maps onto an auction budget or a port contract deadline. It creates "we're all in this" tension without rigged odds. |
| Physics chaos / failure comedy | Medium | Works only if failures come from player actions (dropping a fragile find, crushing a crate with a forklift), never from rigged rolls. This matters for the project's honest-randomness rule. |
| Proximity voice | Weak unless the game is a 3D first-person dock | The current prototype is a 2D/web incremental, so voice is a big scope jump. |
| Watchable reveal | Strong | The container-door and tile-scrape reveal is a natural group "gasp" moment, close to Content Warning's camera payoff and PEAK's watchability. It is the cheapest friendslop ingredient to add. |

- Two co-op flavours exist: **chaotic** (Lethal Company, R.E.P.O., Supermarket Together) and **cozy parallel** (PowerWash, House Flipper 2). A calm incremental reveal game fits the cozy flavour unless physics handling is added.
- Friendslop economics favour a low price ($5–$10), short sessions and a near-zero-wishlist viral launch. That is a different business model from a premium idle/incremental. **[inference]**

### Gaps
- Few primary developer interviews; Zeekerss rarely gives them. The R.E.P.O. "cleaning game" origin quote is unverified.
- No quantitative study isolating proximity voice's effect on virality; the evidence is anecdotal or design commentary.
- RV There Yet? and Mage Arena were not researched in detail.

## Q3. What do players complain about in co-op sims?

### Takeaway
The recurring complaints:
1. Non-host progress is lost, desynced or not saved. "Host-only progress" is the default design and the top source of anger.
2. Desync and lag that worsen as the business grows and NPC counts rise.
3. Disconnects at save points.
4. Weak or punishing solo mode in co-op-first games.
5. Player caps (e.g. 2-player story co-op) and missing cross-play.
6. DDoS, hacking and lobby problems once a game blows up.

### Cited Findings
**Non-host progress and desync**
- Schedule I non-host players report:
  - progression wiped after the host restarts a lobby
  - unlocked regions relocking after crashes
  - suppliers and dealers vanishing from their phone
  - beds and planters shifting position for everyone but the host
  - employee assignments shown as assigned for the host but not for others

  Threads say these issues persisted since launch; the dev prioritised fixing "random kicking". — [Steam: co-op progression desync](https://steamcommunity.com/app/3164500/discussions/1/599650563430319444/); [Steam: multiplayer desync](https://steamcommunity.com/app/3164500/discussions/3/599649349440383309/); [Steam: contact progress desync](https://steamcommunity.com/app/3164500/discussions/1/599661451928595171/)
- House Flipper 2 co-op: the "host always shares the game progress with others", i.e. guests do not advance their own saves. — [Steam guide](https://steamcommunity.com/app/1190970/discussions/0/560232227703642834/)
- PowerWash Simulator 2 explicitly saves Career co-op progress for both players, which is a response to this complaint class. — [co-op.gg](https://www.co-op.gg/game/powerwash-simulator-2)

**Scale-dependent desync and disconnects**
- Supermarket Together players report:
  - "host connection lost" after the end-of-day autosave, sometimes losing a day
  - co-op getting more unstable "as shop got bigger", with desync to unplayable levels

  The dev said stores with more than 70 NPCs at once need a host with a "decent stable connection". — [Steam thread](https://steamcommunity.com/app/2709570/discussions/0/597388550651017225/); [Steam thread](https://steamcommunity.com/app/2709570/discussions/0/4695657936515552307/)
- Supermarket Simulator's co-op launch needed a hotfix for macOS connection errors, save-file issues and ultrawide UI. Players opened a "Multi player bugs since update PLS FIX!" thread. — [Steam thread](https://steamcommunity.com/app/2670630/discussions/0/681860701882481219/); [gamepressure](https://www.gamepressure.com/newsroom/supermarket-simulator-multiplayer-and-coop-explained/z669eb)
- Supermarket Simulator's co-op has no cross-platform play. — [canwecrossplay](https://canwecrossplay.com/is-supermarket-simulator-crossplay/)

**Weak solo mode**
- Lethal Company solo players call it "way too hard and punitive". Many items and monsters are pointless solo. A solo death wipes all scrap, whereas a team keeps scrap if one player survives. — [Steam thread](https://steamcommunity.com/app/1966720/discussions/0/4032473436328346677/); [Steam thread](https://steamcommunity.com/app/1966720/discussions/0/3958162152962608317/)
- "Unenjoyable when played alone" is a defining friendslop trait. — [Wikipedia: Friendslop](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Friendslop)

**Player caps and lobbies**
- PowerWash Simulator 2 story co-op is capped at 2, prompting a "Limited to two players?" thread. — [Steam thread](https://steamcommunity.com/app/2968420/discussions/0/598541260271369788/)
- Schedule I's 4-player cap spawned a 16-player mod. — [GitHub](https://github.com/CyrilZ0817/Schedule-1-Multiplayer-Mod-New-version/)
- After R.E.P.O. "kinda blew up", semiwork asked the community how to build matchmaking lobbies. Friends-only lobbies leave players without friends out. — [PC Gamer](https://www.pcgamer.com/games/horror/after-repo-kinda-blew-up-its-developer-asks-the-community-for-advice-on-how-to-make-matchmaking-lobbies/)

**Abuse at scale**
- R.E.P.O. faced DDoS attacks and hacking attempts at scale. — [Photon blog](https://blog.photonengine.com/r-e-p-o-multiplayer-success-powered-by-photon/)

**Demand without co-op**
- Gas Station Simulator players ask for co-op because the job is "a lot of tasks for one person". This shows co-op is also wanted as workload relief, not just for fun. — [Steam thread](https://steamcommunity.com/app/1149620/discussions/0/599660572366119529/)

### Inferences
- For a progression-heavy sim (upgrades, collections, prestige), **who owns the progress** is the critical co-op design decision. Options:
  - host-only save: cheap, but guests are angry
  - each player keeps their own save (PowerWash 2)
  - shared "company" save plus personal collection rewards
- Dockside's collection and prestige systems would make host-only progress especially painful. Any co-op plan should decide progress ownership first. **[inference]**
- Desync grows with entity count. Business sims scale NPCs and objects over time, so late-game shops break first. A container game with many tiles and items needs bandwidth-cheap state, e.g. syncing tile reveals as events rather than streaming every object. **[inference]**
- All randomness should be rolled on the host or server and broadcast. That keeps every player's view of a reveal identical and the published odds honest. **[inference, aligned with the project's honest-randomness rule]**

### Gaps
- No systematic review-text analysis (e.g. % of negative reviews citing multiplayer) was found for any sim. The evidence here is thread-level.
- I could not open the TCG Card Shop fan-wiki "known issues" list, and it describes a co-op mode that probably does not exist, so it is excluded.

## Q4. What does co-op cost a small Unity team (networking options, extra time, pitfalls), and is it better to ship solo first and add co-op later?

### Takeaway
- **Networking libraries:** free OSS options (FishNet, Mirror, or Unity's Netcode for GameObjects) running over Steam's free P2P relay give a small team co-op with no per-player server bill. Schedule I shipped on FishNet and Lethal Company on NGO. Photon (R.E.P.O., Content Warning) is the fastest to ship but bills by concurrent users.
- **Time cost:** the honest estimate is that co-op multiplies scope rather than adding a feature. Retrofitting ranges from "a free DLC 17 months later" (House Flipper 2) to "impossible with this architecture" (Gas Station Simulator).
- **Ship solo or co-op first:** if co-op is likely, build the architecture for it from the start even if solo ships first.

### Cited Findings

**Networking options in shipped hits**

| Library | Shipped hits | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| FishNet | Schedule I | Mods use "the game's native FishNet implementation" and its Tugboat transport. — [Nexus Mods](https://www.nexusmods.com/schedule1/mods/1753?tab=docs); [s1modding resources](https://s1modding.github.io/docs/moddevs/resources/) |

**Library comparison**
- **Mirror:** the oldest OSS library (UNET heritage). Server-authoritative, with lag compensation, interest management, Steam/Epic transports and WebGL.
- **FishNet:** client-side prediction for transforms and rigidbodies.
- **NGO:** Unity's official high-level library. — [Edgegap](https://edgegap.com/blog/easiest-unity-netcode-for-game-developers); [appwill](https://appwill.co/multiplayer-in-unity-best-networking-solutions-2025/)
- Unity forum, Aug 2025 (CodeSmile): NGO has official support and a Facepunch Steam transport. FishNet is "a solo developer's work… [that] can typically move faster". NGO historically lacked client-side prediction, which matters less for co-op. "for a solo dev what matters the most is accessibility and support." — [Unity Discussions](https://discussions.unity.com/t/so-what-is-the-difference-between-netcode-for-gameobjects-mirror-and-fishnet-which-one-is-better-for-fast-co-op/1678432)

**Steam P2P (cost $0 per player)**
- Steam Datagram Relay: for P2P games "all you need to do… is to use APIs such as CreateListenSocketP2P and ConnectP2P. Steam will take care of everything else." Traffic is relayed, which hides IPs and protects against DoS. The doc states no fees. — [Steamworks docs](https://partner.steamgames.com/doc/features/multiplayer/steamdatagramrelay)
- Free OSS transports exist:
  - FizzySteamworks and FizzyFacepunch for Mirror: Steam NAT punch with relay fallback. — [Mirror docs](https://mirror-networking.gitbook.io/docs/manual/transports/fizzysteamworks-transport)
  - FishySteamworks for FishNet; a Facepunch transport for NGO.

**Common pitfalls (cited above)**
- physics sync (R.E.P.O.)
- non-host save and progress desync (Schedule I)
- host bandwidth at high NPC counts (Supermarket Together, >70 NPCs)

- platform-specific connection bugs (Supermarket Simulator on macOS)
- no cross-play (Supermarket Simulator)
- DDoS and cheating at scale (R.E.P.O.)

### Inferences
- **Cost picture for a small Unity team** (all **[estimate]**):
  - **Licences/servers:** $0 with Steam P2P + FishNet/Mirror/NGO and host-authoritative listen servers.
  - **Photon:** cheap at small scale ($95–$370/month) but scales with success. At 50k CCU on Premium Cloud, $0.29 × 50,000 ≈ $14.5k/month (my arithmetic from the listed price).
  - **Engineering:** roughly 1.5–3× the single-player scope for a systems-heavy sim, going by the anecdotal forum estimates. Lower if the game is designed for networking from the start and reuses a known stack.
  - **QA:** testing doubles at minimum (host/guest × platform × save states).
- **Recommended order** (an inference from the pattern of successes and failures, not a cited rule):
  1. Ship single-player first.
  2. Architect for co-op from day one. Keep all game state in data-driven, serialisable models (Dockside already keeps content in `data/*.json`). Keep randomness on one authority. Route every action through commands that could be sent over a network.
  3. Then add co-op as a free update or DLC paired with a free weekend, as House Flipper 2 did.

  Retrofitting into an architecture that never planned for it is where teams get stuck (Gas Station Simulator).
- **Why co-op day-one is risky for an unproven loop.** The co-op-first hits that exploded (Lethal Company, R.E.P.O., PEAK, Schedule I) were cheap, short-session games where co-op is the point. For an incremental reveal-and-collect game, the TCG Card Shop Simulator path (solo, a strong reveal, streamer-friendly) is the proven lower-risk route. **[inference]**
- **Web-prototype phase:** the current browser prototype cannot test Steam P2P co-op. Co-op should be a Unity-phase decision. A cheap early signal would be "pass-the-mouse" or shared-screen playtests of whether watching a friend's reveal is fun. **[inference]**
