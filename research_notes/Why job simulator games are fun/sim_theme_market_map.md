# Market map: job / business / shop simulator themes on Steam, 2024–2026

Notes as of 3 October 2026. Questions are ordered by the coordinator's priority: saturated vs. open themes, then demand gaps, then asset feasibility, then the slop glut, then tag and genre analyses.

**How to read the numbers (please keep these caveats in the report)**
- **"EN rev"** is the review count from the Steam store search tooltip, collected 3 Oct 2026 with `l=english`. This is **English-language reviews only**, not the all-language total. For globally popular games it undercounts by roughly 1.5–2×. For example, TCG Card Shop Simulator shows 33,978 EN rev against 50,379 all-language from Steam's `appreviews` endpoint the same day, and Supermarket Simulator shows 34,391 EN rev against about 68,341 all-language in SteamSpy's mid-2025 snapshot. The release date shown is the store's current date, which for early-access games is the 1.0 date.
- **Unit estimates** use reviews × ~30, the median ratio for 2022–2026 releases according to [Steam Page Analyzer](https://www.steampageanalyzer.com/blog/steam-review-to-sales-multiplier) and [Gamalytic](https://gamalytic.com/blog/how-to-accurately-estimate-steam-sales). The ratio is about 60× for games near 70% positive and roughly doubles for free games. In Gamalytic's own tests, 77% of its estimates landed within ±30% of the true figure. Applied to EN rev, × 30 gives a rough **floor**. All such figures are labelled "est.".
- **How the sweep worked.** I sent about 95 theme keywords to Steam search, 50 results each, sorted by relevance. I then counted only games whose **name** contains the theme word and that released in 2024 or later, excluding prologues and demos. Because each term returned at most 50 results, release counts are **lower bounds**. Steam rate-limited me partway through, so 3 theme groups were not swept (fish/pet shop, other retail, warehouse/port) and **co-op flags were not collected systematically**. Co-op is stated only where a source says so.
- **UNVERIFIED** marks a claim from a search snippet or aggregator that I could not trace to a primary source.

## Q1. Which themes are saturated and which are open, with evidence? (includes the 2024–2026 release map)

### Takeaway
Shop-sim themes are winner-take-all. One or two early entrants take almost all the reviews, and the clone waves that follow earn a small fraction. Supermarket, card shop, cafe/restaurant/fast food, drug-dealing and internet cafe are saturated: many 2024–26 releases, falling results after the leader, and few games past 1,000 English reviews. Themes that still produced fresh hits in 2025–26 all added a twist: horror or inspection (Shift At Midnight, Quarantine Zone), cleaning with a story (Crime Scene Cleaner, The WereCleaner), delivery and mail (Easy Delivery Co., Cat Mail Co.), nostalgia retail (Retro Rewind) and novelty venues (Waterpark Simulator).

### Inferences
- **Saturated** (avoid unless the twist is strong): supermarket/convenience, card/collectibles shop, cafe/restaurant/fast food (193+ releases, none at 5k EN reviews), bakery, internet cafe, and drug/crime (Schedule I dominates). The pattern is one or two runaway leaders, then dozens of reskins earning about 1–5% of the leader's reviews.
- **Mature franchise lanes** (hard to break into): car mechanic (PlayWay's CMS), farming (Giants' Farming Simulator) and cleaning by power-washing (FuturLab). New entrants in these lanes succeed only with a different fantasy, such as Crime Scene Cleaner within cleaning.
- **Still producing breakouts in 2025–26:** novelty venues, nostalgia retail, delivery and mail, and inspection or "spot the bad customer" horror. All of these are new framings layered on the shop-sim loop rather than new loops.
- Co-op appears in a large share of 2025–26 breakouts: Schedule I, Supermarket Together, Shift At Midnight, and Storage Hunter, which added it at 1.0. Solo-only hits get public requests for co-op. This is a trend inference; I did not collect co-op flags for every title.

### Gaps
- Co-op status, price and developer for each title were not collected systematically because Steam rate-limited the scrape. The co-op status of Fast Food Simulator, Kebab Chefs, Ale & Tale Tavern, Crime Scene Cleaner and PowerWash Simulator 2 is not verified here.
- Three theme groups were not swept: fishing/pet shop, other retail (toy, electronics, video store, gym, barber, tattoo and similar) and warehouse/port logistics. Retro Rewind and Waterpark Simulator come from articles, not the sweep.
- Release counts are lower bounds, capped at 50 search results per keyword. Steam's `total_count` per keyword was not stored.
- Several pawn-shop titles surfaced in news but did not appear in the sweep with review counts: Yacou Games' Pawn Shop Simulator (1.0 Apr 2026) and Lemonade Studio's PawnShop Simulator (1.0 Feb 2026) — [Steam](https://store.steampowered.com/app/3954140/Pawn_Shop_Simulator/), [Steam](https://store.steampowered.com/app/3149100). Their performance is unknown.

## Q2. Which 5–10 themes show demand but have few good games?

### Takeaway
Ranked by demand signal against competition: (1) inspection or "spot the impostor" job sims; (2) storage auction, flea market and treasure-flipping; (3) pawn shop and second-hand appraisal; (4) nostalgia retail such as video stores, record shops and arcades; (5) bookshop and thrift; (6) delivery, mail and packing; (7) novelty leisure venues; (8) co-op versions of proven solo loops. Pharmacy, moving and internet cafe are empty but show no demand.

### Cited Findings
1. **Inspection / "bad customer" job sims.** Shift At Midnight sold close to 1M copies in July 2026 ([GameDiscoverCo](https://newsletter.gamediscover.co/p/july-2026s-top-new-pc-and-console)). Quarantine Zone: The Last Check has 5,788 EN rev since Jan 2026 and Border Pioneer has 1,682 ([Steam search](https://store.steampowered.com/search/?term=border)). XenoFeels, a space-customs sim in the Papers, Please tradition, was among notable Next Fest demos in June 2026 ([gg.deals](https://gg.deals/gaming-news/these-were-the-50-most-played-demos-of-steam-next-fest-june-2026/)). Fewer than 5 inspection titles have 1k+ EN reviews.
2. **Storage auction / flea market / treasure flipping.** Only one name-matched 2024–26 title passed 1k EN reviews: Storage Hunter Simulator at 1,837 and a middling 78% ([Steam search](https://store.steampowered.com/search/?term=storage%20hunter)). Its astragon page calls multiplayer the "most requested feature" ([astragon](https://www.astragon.com/news/detail/storage-hunter-simulator-hits-10-tomorrow)). Several auction games are unreleased (Auction Gambit, Sep 2026; Lost Relics Auction House, 2026; Auction Simulator, 2027), so the lane is filling ([Steam search](https://store.steampowered.com/search/?term=auction)). Storage Hustle and the browser game Container Auction: Puzzle Simulator (Apr 2025) exist too ([Steam](https://store.steampowered.com/app/2529170/Storage_Hustle/); [Playgama](https://playgama.com/game/container-auction)). Dealer's Life 2, a haggling and pawn game, has about 3.9k reviews in SteamSpy's mid-2025 snapshot ([SteamSpy](https://steamspy.com/api.php?request=genre&genre=Simulation)).
3. **Pawn shop and appraisal.** Probably Stolen, a cyberpunk pawnshop sim (tinyBuild / Questing Goose), had 100k wishlists, a demo at 97% of 981 reviews and an 11k+ demo CCU peak; it launches 28 Oct 2026 ([ixbt.games](https://ixbt.games/en/news/2026/09/20/demka-kiberpank-simuliator-prodavca-nelegalnyx-tovarov-stala-xitom-steam-nazvana-data-vyxoda-polnoi-versii-probably-stolen.html); [Steam](https://store.steampowered.com/app/4348910/Probably_Stolen__Cyberpunk_Shopkeeper_Sim/)). GameDiscoverCo saw it at about 10k CCU, #2 on Steam Deck ([GameDiscoverCo](https://newsletter.gamediscover.co/p/what-are-steam-decks-biggest-2026)). Meanwhile the straight 2024–26 pawn sims are weak: Pawnbroker Simulator has 365 EN rev at 69% and Pawn Planet 281 at 71%. Strange Antiquities, an occult antique-shop puzzle game, has 2,352 at 95% ([Steam search](https://store.steampowered.com/search/?term=pawn)).
4. **Nostalgia retail.** Retro Rewind sold 100k in about 5 days and about 200k in two weeks, with 7,892 EN rev at 95% ([TechRaptor](https://techraptor.net/gaming/news/retro-rewind-sales-100k-roadmap)). Media credited nostalgia for the defunct rental business ([Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Retro_Rewind)). Few direct competitors appeared: Retro Arcade Shop Simulator has 244 EN rev and Replay Value: Second Hand Games is upcoming in Nov 2026 ([Steam search](https://store.steampowered.com/search/?term=game%20shop)).
5. **Bookshop and thrift.** Tiny Bookshop (Aug 2025) has 5,029 EN rev at 97% and Bookshop Simulator 1,534 at 92%. Thrifty Business (May 2026) has 1,262 at 97%. Fewer than 10 name-matched releases exist in each theme ([Steam search](https://store.steampowered.com/search/?term=bookshop); [Steam search](https://store.steampowered.com/search/?term=thrift)).
6. **Delivery, mail and packing.** Easy Delivery Co. has 5,719 EN rev at 94%, Cat Mail Co. 3,612 at 94% with 200k+ sold in July 2026, DEAD LETTER DEPT. 1,784 and Parcel Simulator 1,666 ([Steam search](https://store.steampowered.com/search/?term=delivery); [GameDiscoverCo](https://newsletter.gamediscover.co/p/july-2026s-top-new-pc-and-console)). Parcel Simulator grew from 7,000 to 42,000 wishlists before launch, mostly through demos and festivals ([How To Market A Game](https://howtomarketagame.com/2025/08/26/the-demo-effect-from-7000-wishlists-to-42000/)). Zukowski's Dec 2025 list of top job sims includes Parcel Simulator and Wrap House Simulator ([How To Market A Game](https://howtomarketagame.com/2025/12/29/2026-games-of-the-year/)).
7. **Novelty leisure venues.** Waterpark Simulator sold about 250k and $2.3M and was the only one of 38 August-2025 "Simulator" launches to clear $70k ([GameDiscoverCo](https://newsletter.gamediscover.co/p/the-rise-of-the-first-person-sim)). Roadside Research, a roadside alien-research sim, reached 250k wishlists in about 3 months ([same source](https://newsletter.gamediscover.co/p/the-rise-of-the-first-person-sim)).
8. **Co-op versions of solo loops.** Supermarket Together (free, co-op) has review volume comparable to Supermarket Simulator ([Steam search](https://store.steampowered.com/search/?term=supermarket)). Card Shop Simulator Multiplayer reached 1,641 EN rev, the second-best result in a saturated card-shop field. Players of Car Dealer Simulator keep asking for co-op ([Steam discussions](https://steamcommunity.com/app/2404880/discussions/0/684114729915028329/)). Among Steam games with more than $100k lifetime revenue, the share mentioning co-op rose from 3.2% in 2022 to 5.8% in 2023, against only 1.4% to 2.1% of all releases between 2023 and 2025. GameDiscoverCo's conclusion: "making co-op games is hard(er), but those who can reap the dividends" ([GameDiscoverCo, Feb 2026](https://newsletter.gamediscover.co/p/what-game-descriptions-tell-us-about)).
- **Empty without demand:** pharmacy (best: 243 EN rev at 57%), moving (best job-sim entry: 112 at 42%), internet cafe (no 2024–26 title above 846) ([Steam search](https://store.steampowered.com/search/?term=pharmacy)).

### Inferences
- A mystery-container auction game sits at the meeting point of gaps 2 and 3 (auction, appraisal, flipping), with a light link to 1 if bidding rivals or fakes must be read. The lane has one mediocre incumbent (Storage Hunter, 78%), a well-wishlisted adjacent entrant (Probably Stolen) and an unreleased pipeline, so the window is open now but not empty.
- Being *first-person and streamable* plus *one clear twist* matters more than which raw theme is picked. Pawn and pharmacy show that a plain theme plus the shop-sim loop is not enough.
- The co-op premium is real, but it costs a lot: Car Dealer Simulator's developers said retrofitting would mean rebuilding the game. It has to be decided at the start.

### Gaps
- No direct wishlist data per theme. Steam's "Popular Upcoming" and SteamDB wishlist ranks were not reachable (rate limit, Cloudflare).
- Rival auction-sim wishlist counts are unknown (Auction Gambit, Lost Relics Auction House, Auction Simulator, and Pawn Shop Simulator by Yacou). Probably Stolen's launch result is unknown (launches 28 Oct 2026).

## Q3. Asset feasibility: which themes have good ready-made assets for a small Unity team, and which need custom art?

Real-world retail and service themes are well covered by store packs: supermarket, cafe, gas station, bar or tavern, motel, pharmacy, laundromat, bookstore, junkyard and warehouse. Complete supermarket-sim code templates are also sold, which partly explains the clone flood. The hard parts are a large catalogue of distinct sellable items (cards, antiques, VHS tapes, auction loot), believable customer NPCs, and a look that does not read as a recognisable asset flip, especially Synty's low-poly style.

Players link Synty's low-poly look with "asset flip vibes" after several games shipped the same models and effects — [search summary of Steam discussions](https://steamcommunity.com/app/2382520/discussions/0/506200271931576244).

### Inferences
- **Easy with store assets:** supermarket, convenience store, cafe, gas station, motel, bar or tavern, pharmacy, laundromat, bookstore, junkyard, warehouse and office jobs.
- **Medium effort:** pawn and antique, flea market and storage auction. Rooms are covered by the antique and junkyard packs, but the **items** are the product: hundreds of distinct appraisable objects. That means stacking several prop packs (Shops' 967 props, Office, Farm, Construction, antiques) plus custom hero items.
- **Hard or custom:** card shop (needs original card art in volume), nostalgia retail (fake VHS or game box art in volume, which AI art would undercut; see Q4), and any stylised or IP-adjacent setting such as Probably Stolen's cyberpunk pixel art.
- The cheaper a theme is to build from store packs and templates, the more clones it attracts (supermarket being the clearest case). Cheap-to-build themes are crowded for exactly that reason.

### Gaps
- I did not verify whether Synty's City or Town packs include a usable gas station, or whether any Synty pack fits a pawn shop.
- Asset prices for the non-Synty packs were mostly not captured.
- I found no data on how strongly specific asset styles affect sales of job sims in particular.

## Q4. What does the "simulator slop" glut look like, and how do players tell quality apart?

### Takeaway
The glut is real and measurable. In August 2025, 38 "Simulator"-titled games launched on Steam and only one cleared $70k gross. About 1 in 5 Steam releases in 2025 disclosed generative AI, and those games got markedly fewer reviews. Viral concepts now attract same-name clones within days. Players filter on a clear novel hook, streamer clips, demo and review scores, developer track record and update cadence, and they penalise disclosed AI and recognisable flipped assets.

### Cited Findings
- 38 games with "Simulator" in the title launched on Steam in **August 2025**. Only Waterpark Simulator passed $70k gross; the runners-up were Returns Outlet Simulator ($69k) and Parisian Brasserie Simulator ($62k) — [GameDiscoverCo, 23 Sep 2025](https://newsletter.gamediscover.co/p/the-rise-of-the-first-person-sim).
- Earlier, GameDiscoverCo reported a median of about $70k gross and 5,000–7,000 copies for "X Simulator" titles released since July 2023 — [WN Hub, 2 Jul 2024](https://wnhub.io/news/stores-and-publishing/item-44009). **Conflict:** the August 2025 data (37 of 38 below $70k) implies the 2024 median was taken over a filtered set, such as games with some minimum reviews, not all releases. Treat $70k as a "games that got traction" median.
- GameDiscoverCo sees "clustering of cheaper or mid-price games around breakout microgenres", with first-person sims the obvious example — [GameDiscoverCo July 2026](https://newsletter.gamediscover.co/p/july-2026s-top-new-pc-and-console).
- Needle In A Haystack Simulator went viral in late August 2026. Within days, 10+ similarly named clones appeared ("A Needle in a Haystack", "CO-OP Needle Hunt", "Find the Needle" and others), some with logos "drenched in that ChatGPT sheen" — [Kotaku, 17 Sep 2026](https://kotaku.com/the-proliferation-of-copycats-on-steam-makes-discovering-original-games-like-finding-a-needle-in-a-haystack-2000734794). The original's developer, Studio Bitdot, apologised "to the Steam storefront", and press called the trend "the Robloxification of Steam" — [GamesRadar](https://www.gamesradar.com/games/simulation/the-robloxification-of-steam-creator-of-viral-game-apologizes-after-slop-rip-offs-flood-valves-store/) (headline and snippet; full text not retrieved).
- A player complaint thread on Steam: "My discovery list is FILLED with all these One week project slop trash shovelware Shop sims that are ALL the same", and "asset flip games people pay a week to make and flood the store" (May 2025) — [Steam Universe group](https://steamcommunity.com/groups/steamuniverse/discussions/0/591772052728332551/).
- Supermarket Simulator's developer had to state that look-alike games such as "Internet cafe & Supermarket simulator" were from other studios imitating its concept (Jun 2024) — [Steam discussions](https://steamcommunity.com/app/2670630/discussions/0/4413046572166283625/).
- **AI disclosure.** 7,818 Steam titles disclosed generative AI (about 7% of the library, up roughly 681% year-on-year), and about 20% of 2025 releases did — [Tom's Hardware](https://www.tomshardware.com/video-games/pc-gaming/1-in-5-steam-games-released-in-2025-use-generative-ai-up-nearly-700-percent-year-on-year-7-818-titles-disclose-genai-asset-usage-7-percent-of-entire-steam-library). The analyst behind the data was not identified in the retrieved text. A Game Oracle study (Ross Burton) of about 10,000 games released Jan–Oct 2025 found about 21% carried an AI disclosure, and that comparable games with a disclosure got about 52.6% fewer reviews (an average of 4 against 7 in the first month) — [Digital Citizen](https://www.digitalcitizen.life/steam-games-that-disclose-ai-use-may-sell-far-less-new-study-finds/); [Windows Central](https://www.windowscentral.com/gaming/pc-gaming/ai-game-development-stigma-study).
- **PlayWay's model.** The Polish publisher creates many Steam pages for concepts, tests them with ads and wishlists, and builds only those that respond. It once led all publishers with 29 games in Steam's top-wishlisted list and had 114 games in its pipeline — [Game World Observer, Sep 2021](https://gameworldobserver.com/2021/09/03/how-publisher-playway-strives-by-promoting-dozens-of-steam-games-that-might-never-come-out); [Game World Observer, Apr 2021](https://gameworldobserver.com/2021/04/27/playway-top-steam-publishers-by-wishlists-outshining-ea-square-enix-sega). GameDiscoverCo praised its niche-targeting but criticised misleading "target gameplay" trailers made before development and the use of opaque subsidiaries — [GameDiscoverCo, Apr 2021](https://newsletter.gamediscover.co/p/what-playway-should-and-shouldnt). Hits from the network include House Flipper, Car Mechanic Simulator, Thief Simulator, Drug Dealer Simulator and Crime Scene Cleaner — same sources; [Steam news](https://store.steampowered.com/news/app/1040200/view/523103429639275286).
- **What separates winners,** according to GameDiscoverCo: a "catchy concept, easy to grok gameplay and supreme streamer-compatibility", with entertainment coming from amusing job failures — [GameDiscoverCo](https://newsletter.gamediscover.co/p/the-rise-of-the-first-person-sim). TCG Card Shop's success rested on "broad influencer appeal" and cultural timing (Pokémon card grading up to 43% in 2023 from 17% in 2018) — [GameDiscoverCo](https://newsletter.gamediscover.co/p/tcg-card-shop-simulator-the-second).
- **Signals players use, as seen in community threads:** developer identity and history, update frequency ("other games/studios frequently updating their games to give a better experience"), review scores, trailers, and recognising flipped assets — [Steam discussions](https://steamcommunity.com/app/2670630/discussions/0/4413046572166283625/); [Steam Universe](https://steamcommunity.com/groups/steamuniverse/discussions/0/591772052728332551/). Free demos act as public proof: Shift At Midnight's demo had about 4.8k reviews at 99% before launch — [indiegame.com](https://indiegame.com/en/archives/31766).

### Inferences
- The flood sits at the bottom of the market (one-week, $5–10, AI capsule art, same-name titles). It harms discovery, but it does not change the outcomes for distinctive games: winners in 2025–26 still sold 100k–1M.
- Quality signals the team controls: an honest demo released early (also the main wishlist driver; see Q5), no AI art disclosure, a distinctive art direction even when built from packs, visible update cadence, and a hook that fits in one sentence and makes a funny clip.

### Gaps
- I found no breakdown of AI-disclosure rates by genre, so I can't say whether simulators disclose more than other genres.
- I found no player-survey data on how buyers tell sims apart; the signals above come from forum anecdotes and analyst commentary.
- The full GamesRadar article text was not retrieved.

## Q5. What do Steam tag and genre analyses say about this lane's performance and typical outcomes?

### Takeaway
Simulation is a consistently strong Steam genre: #2 in 2025 by the share of games reaching 1,000 reviews (4.1%). The typical outcome is still tiny, though. Most "Simulator" launches gross well under $70k, and breakouts depend on a hook that streamers enjoy, a strong demo and wishlist conversion. Chris Zukowski explicitly argues that indies underrate job sims.

### Cited Findings
- In 2025, 43 of 1,048 simulation games (4.1%) reached 1,000+ reviews, up from 3.76% in 2024. Simulation ranked **#2** among genres in 2025, up from #3. Zukowski uses "Simulation + Management" tags and gives Car Dealer Simulator as an example, and notes that "9 out of the 10 genres at the top of the list in 2025 were exactly the same" as in prior years — [How To Market A Game, Jan 2026](https://howtomarketagame.com/2026/01/27/what-the-hell-happened-in-2025/).
- "Steam fans really really like simulating a job, particularly if it is blue collar." Simulation "saw a major surge" in early 2025 on the back of Supermarket Simulator and Schedule I — [How To Market A Game, Apr 2025](https://howtomarketagame.com/2025/04/11/the-hit-games-of-early-2025/).
- Zukowski's "Job simulator" section in his Dec 2025 games-of-the-year post:
  - "Too many Indies turn their nose up at Job simulators saying they are AI-generated capsule slop, or money grabs, or jank junk… Players really like these games… Just because they aren't 'cool' doesn't mean there isn't potential here."
  - Top job sims: Parcel Simulator ($17.99), Wrap House Simulator ($14.99), Lushfoil Photography Sim ($14.99), Laundry Store Simulator ($11.99) and TransSiberian Railway Simulator ($19.99).
  - On the cusp: Console Shop Simulator, Medieval Crafter: Blacksmith and Bakery Cafe Simulator.
  - He cites David Wehle's narrative-plus-job-sim approach (We Harvest Shadows: 220k wishlists in 3 months) as the indie-authenticity model.
  - Sources: [How To Market A Game, Dec 2025](https://howtomarketagame.com/2025/12/29/2026-games-of-the-year/); [How To Market A Game, Nov 2024](https://howtomarketagame.com/2024/11/18/how-we-harvest-shadows-220000-wishlists-in-3-months/).
- Demos drive wishlists: Parcel Simulator went from 7,000 wishlists (Feb 2025) to over 42,000 at its June launch — [How To Market A Game](https://howtomarketagame.com/2025/08/26/the-demo-effect-from-7000-wishlists-to-42000/).
- Roadside Research reached 250k+ wishlists in about 3 months; its demo added 31k wishlists and peaked at 2,384 CCU. Waterpark Simulator's Week-1 wishlist conversion was 11× the median — [GameDiscoverCo, Sep 2025](https://newsletter.gamediscover.co/p/the-rise-of-the-first-person-sim).
- GameDiscoverCo's 2024 top new games by copies sold: Supermarket Simulator #7 and TCG Card Shop Simulator #16, both over 1M — [GameDiscoverCo, Dec 2024](https://newsletter.gamediscover.co/p/revealed-the-top-new-pc-and-console).
- In 2026 the "shop" keyword is growing in GameDiscoverCo's Steam keyword trends, which it reads as the "Recettear" fantasy of running a business — [ingamenews summary](https://www.ingamenews.com/2026/02/gamediscoverco-announces-steam-keyword.html) (page now returns HTTP 410; summary only, **UNVERIFIED**).
- AppMagic reports that Steam simulation has shifted toward "very specific fantasies" rather than broad professions. It cites Car Dealer Simulator at over $11.7M from 658K copies and Megastore Simulator at over $1.8M from 143K — [AppMagic](https://appmagic.rocks/blog/steam-simulation-games-2026/?hl=en) (via search snippet; the page did not render). **Caution:** the same snippet's Storage Hunter figure conflicts with Steam review data (see Q1), so treat AppMagic figures as **UNVERIFIED**. Car Dealer Simulator's 4,532 EN reviews would imply roughly 136k+ est. units at 30×, which is hard to reconcile with 658K unless its non-English share is very large.
- Co-op tailwind: four co-op games (R.E.P.O., PEAK, Schedule I and RV There Yet?) were in Steam's 2025 top 10 premium games by copies sold — [AppMagic friendslop article, via search summary](https://appmagic.rocks/blog/friendslop-steam-games-2025) (**UNVERIFIED** detail).
- In 2025 only 6.5% of the roughly 21,000 Steam games GameDiscoverCo analysed passed $100k lifetime revenue — [GameDiscoverCo, Feb 2026](https://newsletter.gamediscover.co/p/what-game-descriptions-tell-us-about).
- Cabin Crew Life Simulator (2025) earned its developers over US$100,000 — [Wikipedia](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Job_simulation_game).

### Inferences
- **Realistic planning range for a small team's job or shop sim.** Median outcome: low thousands of copies (EN-review data shows most name-matched 2024–26 releases under 300 reviews). A good outcome: 1k–5k reviews, roughly 30k–150k est. units. A breakout: 200k–1M+. The breakouts all had a viral one-sentence hook, a demo that built 40k–250k wishlists, and usually co-op or a strong streamer moment.
- Price clusters at $10–20 (Zukowski's list $11.99–19.99; Waterpark $13; Shift At Midnight $10; Cat Mail Co. $15).

### Gaps
- I did not retrieve a dedicated Zukowski or GameDiscoverCo study of the "Job Simulator" tag as such, with median wishlists or sales per tag. The figures above are genre-level or for "Simulator"-titled games.
- VG Insights and Gamalytic per-game pages are JavaScript-rendered and Gamalytic's API needs a paid key, so I could not pull their estimates directly.
- I did not check the PC Gamer "Steam week in review" series or Reddit r/gamedev threads in depth; searches did not surface relevant items.
